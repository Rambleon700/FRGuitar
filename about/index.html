The Fretwork Philosophy

Theory isn’t the point. The music is.

Knowing a chord shape is useful. Understanding why it works opens doors across the entire fretboard.

Most traditional music theory programs force students through traditional sheet music and classical concepts before ever touching their instrument.

We keep explanations visual, plain, and directly connected to what you can hear and feel under your fingers.
