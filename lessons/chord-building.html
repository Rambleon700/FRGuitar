Discover why three simple notes create major bright sounds or minor dark moods.

What is a Triad?

Most standard guitar chords are built using three specific notes stacked together, known as a triad:

  • Root (1): The anchor note that gives the chord its name.
  • Third (3): Determines whether the chord sounds Major or Minor.
  • Fifth (5): Provides structural stability.
