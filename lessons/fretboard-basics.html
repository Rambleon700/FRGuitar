Lesson 01

Meet Your Fretboard

Six strings, twelve notes, and a logical pattern that repeats. Let's make sense of the neck.

1. Standard Tuning

From thickest string (6th) to thinnest string (1st), the open strings are tuned to:

E — A — D — G — B — E

2. The Half Step Rule

Moving up one fret on the guitar equals a half step (semi-tone). Moving two frets equals a whole step.

Notice that between B & C and E & F, there are no sharp/flat notes in between. Everywhere else, a sharp (#) or flat (b) note sits between natural notes.
